## 🚀 Selected Research Projects

### Design-Aware Robust MAP Borrowing for Simon Two-Stage Trials
*Rice University · Department of Statistics | 2026–Present*

- Developing a **design-aware robust meta-analytic-predictive (rMAP)** framework to borrow information from historical Simon two-stage trials.
- Deriving **design-unbiased and conditional-likelihood estimators** to address selection-induced bias in historical evidence.
- Evaluating **Type I error, power, bias, RMSE, and effective sample size (ESS)** using exact enumeration under agreement and conflict between prior and current data.

### GFB: A Cross-Fitted Graph-Fused Borrowing Framework for Basket Trials
*UNC–Chapel Hill · Department of Biostatistics | 2025–2026*

- Developed **GFB**, a cross-fitted graph-fused framework for basket-specific inference in oncology trials.
- Designed safeguards against overborrowing across dissimilar patient subgroups.
- Applied sample-splitting and cross-fitting to separate model selection from statistical inference.
- Benchmarked GFB against **BHM, EXNEX, RoBoT, and adaptive LASSO**, and applied it to imatinib and vemurafenib trials.

### Quality Assurance for AI-Assisted Systematic Reviews
*UNC–Chapel Hill · Department of Biostatistics | 2025–2026*

- Conducted meta-analysis using weighted models and forest plots to investigate heterogeneity across studies.
- Developed a **Wald Sequential Probability Ratio Test (SPRT)** table for comparing AI and human review decisions.
- Assessed review performance using predictive metrics, including **PPV and NPV**.

### Longitudinal Transcriptomics of Respiratory Infection
*UNC–Chapel Hill · Department of Biostatistics | 2024–2025*

- Integrated gene-expression data across studies and experimental platforms using **R**.
- Applied regression modeling to standardize outcomes and investigated longitudinal immune-response trajectories.
- Contributed to research on the **HR-VILAGE-3K3M** systems-immunity dataset.
