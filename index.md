## Selected Research

### Design-Aware Robust MAP Borrowing for Simon Two-Stage Trials
*Rice University · 2026–Present*

Developing a design-aware rMAP framework for borrowing information from historical Simon two-stage trials. This work considers design-unbiased and conditional-likelihood estimation to address selection-induced bias, and evaluates Type I error, power, bias, RMSE, and effective sample size through exact enumeration.

### GFB: A Cross-Fitted Graph-Fused Borrowing Framework for Basket Trials
*UNC–Chapel Hill · 2025–2026*

Developed a cross-fitted graph-fused approach to basket-specific inference in oncology trials. The framework uses safeguards against overborrowing across dissimilar subgroups and separates model selection from inference using sample splitting and cross-fitting. Evaluated performance against Bayesian hierarchical models, EXNEX, RoBoT, and adaptive LASSO, with applications to imatinib and vemurafenib trials.

### Quality Assurance for AI-Assisted Systematic Reviews
*UNC–Chapel Hill · 2025–2026*

Applied meta-analysis and forest plots to assess heterogeneity across studies. Developed a Wald sequential probability ratio test (SPRT) comparison framework for AI-assisted versus human review decisions, and evaluated predictive performance using positive and negative predictive values.

### Longitudinal Transcriptomics of Respiratory Infection
*UNC–Chapel Hill · 2024–2025*

Integrated gene-expression data across studies and experimental platforms using R, standardized outcomes through regression-based approaches, and studied longitudinal immune-response trajectories. Contributed to the HR-VILAGE-3K3M systems-immunity dataset project.
